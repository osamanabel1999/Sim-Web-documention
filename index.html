New Feature • Weather Training

Ultimate Weather Engine & Custom Scenarios

Take complete control of the flight environment in MSFS 2020 and MSFS 2024. The new Weather Trainer lets you build your own conditions, reproduce challenging weather situations, and prepare for demanding training flights directly from your device.

1. Build Your Own Weather – Layer by Layer Create a custom weather environment from scratch instead of relying on a fixed preset. New Feature • Traffic & Situational Awareness

Interactive TCAS Trainer & Airport Operations

Turn ordinary flights into focused traffic-awareness exercises. The new TCAS & Traffic Injection tools let you create realistic encounters in the simulator, while Airport Operations adds runway traffic and surprise incursion scenarios for takeoff and approach training.

Custom AI Traffic Injection Add aircraft to your flight environment and choose the aircraft type, relative direction (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, or NW), distance, altitude, airspeed, and vertical speed. Set up runway-occupancy conflicts and unexpected runway incursions, including aircraft entering the runway while you are preparing for departure or on final approach. These scenarios help you practice visual scanning, situational awareness, rejected-takeoff decisions, and go-around judgment in a controlled virtual environment.

MSFS Connection Guide

A clean, step-by-step guide to connecting the SimulatorStation mobile app with Microsoft Flight Simulator on your PC.

Before you begin

  • Have Microsoft Flight Simulator running on your PC.
  • Keep your mobile device and PC on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • If you use SimBrief, IVAO, or VATSIM, have your relevant ID ready.
  • Instructor Session: If you are flying in a shared session with an instructor, ensure both the Pilot and Instructor enter the exact same Session ID on the connection page.
Step 1

Download the Bridge Files

Download the Simulator Station Bridge ZIP file to your Windows PC. This application is required to establish communication between your simulator and your device.

https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1YsBM1cZVrXWPpPl8QIfHqTyHdsQTJfak?usp=sharing

* Copy the link above and open it in your PC browser to download the required files.

After downloading, extract the ZIP file to a convenient location on your PC. Inside the extracted folder, you will find two main folders: MSFS and X-Plane. Since you are using MSFS, open the MSFS folder.

Step 2

Run the Bridge Server

Inside the MSFS folder, you will find two executable files. It is crucial to understand the function of each:

  • SimulatorStation_Bridge.v1.0.5.exe This is the primary connection file. You must run this file and leave the window app running in the background to control the simulator from your device.
  • BroadcastMSFS.exe Run this file ONLY if you are flying as a Pilot in a shared session with an Instructor. If you are flying solo or acting as an instructor, do not open this file.
Step 3

Configure Head Tracker (OpenTrack)

To use the revolutionary Head Tracker feature, you must install and configure OpenTrack on your PC.

https://github.com/opentrack/opentrack/releases/download/opentrack-2026.1.0/opentrack-2026.1.0-win32-setup.exe

* Copy the link above and open it in your PC browser to download OpenTrack.

Once installed, open OpenTrack and configure the main dashboard settings as follows:

  • Input Select UDP over network.
  • Port Setting Click the hammer icon next to Input and ensure the Port is set exactly to 4242.
  • Output Select freetrack 2.0 Enhanced.

Next, click on Options at the bottom right. Match your settings exactly as shown in the image below. Crucial: Ensure that "Center at startup" is unchecked/disabled. Once verified, click OK and then click Start to activate tracking!

Step 4

Get Your Hoppie ACARS Logon Code

To unlock the full potential of the ACARS & CPDLC features, you need a personal Logon Code from Hoppie's system. Follow these simple steps to generate and connect it.

https://www.hoppie.nl/acars/system/register.html

* Click the button above to copy the link and automatically open the registration page.

  • 01 Register On the website, fill in your full name and email address. Answer the simple anti-abuse riddle (e.g., type "dog" for man's best friend), check the "I will behave" box, and click Register.
  • 02 Check Your Email You will immediately receive an email containing your unique ACARS Logon Code. Copy this code securely (check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within seconds).
  • 03 Connect in App Open the Simulator Station app, navigate to the ACARS page, paste your code into the Logon Code field, and select your network (VAT or IVA) to establish the connection.
Step 5

Connect & Verify

Open the Simulator Station app on your device. Once the app detects the bridge server running on your PC, the red "Disconnected" banner at the top of the screen will automatically turn green and display "Connected".

App Walkthrough & Features

A detailed visual guide exploring Simulator Station's core control screens.

App Setup

Connect Your Accounts

Input your PC's IP Address to establish the local connection. You can also securely enter your SimBrief ID and your IVAO / VATSIM ID to enable live weather, flight plans, and network traffic within the app. Click Connect when ready.

Positioning & Navigation

Dynamic Control & World Exploration

1. Dynamic Approach Setup Type any airport ICAO code to instantly load its operational data. From the Runway tab, you can select your active runway, dial in your target airspeed, and click on any visual approach pattern (like Left Base, Right Downwind, or a 15nm final). Your aircraft will instantly teleport and align perfectly.

2. Interactive Airport Charts Switch over to the Airport Chart tab to view a meticulously detailed, fully drawn layout of the airport, displaying all runways and taxiways. Spot an aircraft icon at a specific gate? Simply tap it, and your plane will instantly spawn perfectly parked at that exact gate.

3. Advanced Navaids Teleport Search for any navigational aid (VOR or NDB) worldwide to instantly pull up its frequency, elevation, and precise coordinates. Dial in your desired radial, heading, distance, altitude, and speed, then click Teleport. Your aircraft will instantly spawn at the exact pinpointed location in the sky, fully configured and ready to fly.

4. Scenic World Tour Explore over 30 of the world's most iconic and breathtaking landmarks. Simply set your desired target speed, select a destination like the Great Pyramids or the Eiffel Tower, and click FLY HERE. The app instantly teleports your aircraft to enjoy stunning scenic views without the hassle of planning a full route.

Flight Operations

Precision Performance Calculator (T/O & LDG)

Simulator Station features a highly accurate, airline-grade performance calculator that meticulously computes your Take-Off and Landing data based on real-world physics and conditions.

1. Take-Off Data Entry Input your specific aircraft type, gross weight, configuration, anti-ice status, and exact runway conditions (length, slope, heading, elevation). Add precise meteorological data including QNH, exact temperature, and wind direction/speed to ensure maximum realism and safety before departure.

2. Landing Data Entry (LDA) Prepare for a safe arrival by setting your landing weight, Autobrake settings, and reverser operational status. Combine this with the destination airport's elevation, runway condition, and live weather to dynamically calculate your required stopping margins.

3. Highly Accurate T/O Results & V-Speeds Hit calculate to generate impeccably precise V-speeds (V1, VR, V2) and Flex Temperatures. The app also determines your precise flap and slat retraction speeds (F, S, Green Dot). Best of all, the intuitive visual runway diagram displays exactly where on the runway your aircraft will hit these critical speeds relative to the total runway length (TORA).

4. Precise Landing Distances & Overrun Warnings Review your precise Approach Speed (VAPP) and Minimum Selectable Speed (VLS). The calculator actively compares your Actual Distance against the Factored Distance to tell you exactly how much runway will remain. If the runway is too short for your weight and weather conditions, a dynamic red OVERRUN WARNING ensures you abort the approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about Simulator Station.

Why is the app showing "Disconnected" and refusing to connect?

Ensure that the Bridge server is actively running on your PC and that your mobile device and PC are on the exact same Wi-Fi network. Check that your Windows Firewall isn't blocking the connection. Crucial Step: Verify that you entered the correct PC IP Address in the app (local IPs can change upon router restarts) and ensure you are running the latest version of the Bridge.

Does the app support the new MSFS 2024?

Yes, absolutely! Simulator Station is built and optimized to fully support both MSFS 2020 and the newly released MSFS 2024 with maximum efficiency.

Why isn't my flight plan loading from SimBrief?

Ensure you have entered your correct SimBrief ID on the app's Connection page. Most importantly, you must click "Generate Flight" on the SimBrief website for your current flight before opening the Flight Plan tab in the app.

Does the map display live VATSIM and IVAO traffic?

Yes! Once you enter your VATSIM or IVAO ID on the Connection page, you can instantly view live network traffic. You can seamlessly toggle the traffic display on or off directly from the map's left-hand filter menu.

Can I use the Teleport feature while mid-air?

Yes, mid-air teleportation is fully supported. However, always ensure you configure an appropriate target Altitude and target Airspeed in the Teleport settings before executing the jump to prevent your aircraft from stalling upon arrival.
