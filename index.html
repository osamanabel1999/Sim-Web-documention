MSFS Connection Guide

A clean, step-by-step guide to connecting the SimulatorStation mobile app with Microsoft Flight Simulator on your PC.

Before you begin

  • Have Microsoft Flight Simulator running on your PC.
  • Keep your mobile device and PC on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • If you use SimBrief, IVAO, or VATSIM, have your relevant ID ready.
Step 1

Download the Bridge File

Download the SimulatorStation Bridge ZIP file to your Windows PC. Extract the downloaded ZIP folder before continuing.

https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/12HAVgD_xcHbJzZD-UvzIo9G9aFN1IKEK?usp=sharing

* Please copy the link above and open it in your web browser to download the file.

After downloading: Open the ZIP file → extract it to a convenient location on your PC → open the extracted folder.

The file you need to run is: SimulatorStation_Bridge.v1.exe
Step 2

Run the Bridge Server

Open the extracted folder and double-click SimulatorStation_Bridge.v1.exe to start the bridge server.

  • 01 Open the extracted folder Locate the Bridge server files.
  • 02 Run SimulatorStation_Bridge.v1.exe Double-click the executable.
  • 03 Keep the black command window open Leave it running in the background while you fly.
Important: The bridge server must remain open in the background to maintain communication between MSFS and the mobile app.

Next, make sure your phone/tablet and the PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
Step 3

Connect & Verify

Open the Simulator Station app on your device. Once the app detects the bridge server running on your PC, the red "Disconnected" banner at the top of the screen will automatically turn green and display "Connected".

App Walkthrough & Features

A detailed visual guide exploring Simulator Station's core control screens.

App Setup

Connect Your Accounts

Input your PC's IP Address to establish the local connection. You can also securely enter your SimBrief ID and your IVAO / VATSIM ID to enable live weather, flight plans, and network traffic within the app. Click Connect when ready.

Positioning & Navigation

Dynamic Control & World Exploration

1. Dynamic Approach Setup Type any airport ICAO code to instantly load its operational data. From the Runway tab, you can select your active runway, dial in your target airspeed, and click on any visual approach pattern (like Left Base, Right Downwind, or a 15nm final). Your aircraft will instantly teleport and align perfectly.

2. Interactive Airport Charts Switch over to the Airport Chart tab to view a meticulously detailed, fully drawn layout of the airport, displaying all runways and taxiways. Spot an aircraft icon at a specific gate? Simply tap it, and your plane will instantly spawn perfectly parked at that exact gate.

3. Advanced Navaids Teleport Search for any navigational aid (VOR or NDB) worldwide to instantly pull up its frequency, elevation, and precise coordinates. Dial in your desired radial, heading, distance, altitude, and speed, then click Teleport. Your aircraft will instantly spawn at the exact pinpointed location in the sky, fully configured and ready to fly.

4. Scenic World Tour Explore over 30 of the world's most iconic and breathtaking landmarks. Simply set your desired target speed, select a destination like the Great Pyramids or the Eiffel Tower, and click FLY HERE. The app instantly teleports your aircraft to enjoy stunning scenic views without the hassle of planning a full route.

Navigation & Radar

Interactive Map & Live Teleportation

1. Universal Search & Smart Filters Use the top search bar to instantly locate any ICAO, Callsign, Navaid, or active flight. The comprehensive left menu allows you to toggle live VATSIM/IVAO traffic, Airports, Waypoints, Airways, Holds, and your exact Flight Path on or off to perfectly declutter your view.

2. Dynamic Weather & Map Layers Click the layer icon on the right to completely transform your map environment. Switch between Dark and Satellite views, and instantly overlay real-time aviation weather data including Precipitation, Rain, Temp, Wind, Airway Segments, and stunning Aurora visuals.

3. Instant Map Teleportation Want to practice a specific scenario? Click Teleport from the left menu, tap absolutely anywhere on the map, enter your desired Altitude, Speed, and Heading, and hit Send Teleport. Your aircraft will seamlessly spawn at those exact coordinates in a fraction of a second.

4. Interactive Navaids Tap any VOR or NDB on the map to pull up its exact frequency, elevation, power range, and coordinates. With a single click on Teleport To Navaid, you can instantly position your aircraft directly over the station.

5. Ultimate Airport Database Click any airport icon on the map to unlock a massive database. Instantly access real-world ATC frequencies, highly detailed runway specs (length, width, surface type, lighting, bearing), plus live METAR, TAF, and ATIS (text and voice) for complete situational awareness.

Flight Plan

Live SimBrief Integration

No need to manually type your route. The app automatically fetches data from your most recent SimBrief flight plan. You get instant access to your Departure and Arrival airports, block times, cruising altitude, live METAR, TAF, and complete NOTAMs data in a clean, scrollable view.

Ground Operations

Interactive Pushback Control

Take complete command of ground operations without ever touching your PC. Connect the tug and use intuitive one-touch controls to push back straight, or precisely turn the tail left or right to align with the taxiway.

Flight Operations

Precision Performance Calculator (T/O & LDG)

Simulator Station features a highly accurate, airline-grade performance calculator that meticulously computes your Take-Off and Landing data based on real-world physics and conditions.

1. Take-Off Data Entry Input your specific aircraft type, gross weight, configuration, anti-ice status, and exact runway conditions (length, slope, heading, elevation). Add precise meteorological data including QNH, exact temperature, and wind direction/speed to ensure maximum realism and safety before departure.

2. Landing Data Entry (LDA) Prepare for a safe arrival by setting your landing weight, Autobrake settings, and reverser operational status. Combine this with the destination airport's elevation, runway condition, and live weather to dynamically calculate your required stopping margins.

3. Highly Accurate T/O Results & V-Speeds Hit calculate to generate impeccably precise V-speeds (V1, VR, V2) and Flex Temperatures. The app also determines your precise flap and slat retraction speeds (F, S, Green Dot). Best of all, the intuitive visual runway diagram displays exactly where on the runway your aircraft will hit these critical speeds relative to the total runway length (TORA).

4. Precise Landing Distances & Overrun Warnings Review your precise Approach Speed (VAPP) and Minimum Selectable Speed (VLS). The calculator actively compares your Actual Distance against the Factored Distance to tell you exactly how much runway will remain. If the runway is too short for your weight and weather conditions, a dynamic red OVERRUN WARNING ensures you abort the approach.

Virtual Crew

Interactive Checklists & Audio Briefings

Elevate your simulation to a professional multi-crew environment. Simulator Station introduces a fully integrated Virtual Co-Pilot to assist you throughout every phase of your flight.

1. Interactive Voice Checklists Select your aircraft type (e.g., A320 or B737) and choose your current phase of flight (Before Start, Taxi, Line Up, Approach, etc.). The Virtual Pilot will actively read the checklist aloud and execute it with you step-by-step, perfectly mirroring real-world cockpit procedures and ensuring no critical steps are missed.

2. Professional Audio Briefings The app dynamically gathers your runway data, precise Take-Off/Landing speeds, and live weather conditions to compile a comprehensive brief. Simply tap START AUDIO, and your Virtual Co-Pilot will deliver a complete, professional, and immersive audio briefing for either your departure or arrival, exactly as you would hear on a real flight deck.

Smart EFB

Integrated Electronic Flight Bag (iPad)

Simulator Station comes equipped with a fully functional, built-in Electronic Flight Bag (EFB) that mirrors a real-world cockpit tablet. The EFB gives you instant access to four specialized aviation applications: Airport WX, WX Charts, NOTAMs, and a digital Scratchpad.

1. Aviation Weather Charts (WX Charts) Generate highly detailed, real-world meteorological charts on the fly. Simply select your desired Chart Type (such as Upper Air Wind/Temp or SIGWX), choose your geographical Region, and set your specific Parameters (Forecast Time and Flight Level). Click View Weather Chart to instantly render professional aviation weather overlays for your route.

2. Global NOTAMs Database Search for any airport ICAO code worldwide to instantly pull up its active NOTAMs. The app intelligently categorizes the data (All, Aerodrome, Nav, Obstacle) and clearly displays the effective times, expiration times, and active status. You can easily read the detailed text and even copy it directly to your clipboard for flight planning.

3. Authentic Digital Scratchpad Ditch the pen and paper. The EFB includes a highly authentic digital Scratchpad featuring four dedicated, real-world templates: Takeoff, Landing, Holding, and CRAFT (Clearances). These templates are meticulously designed to mirror actual pilot notes, allowing you to seamlessly jot down and reference critical V-speeds, altitudes, and ATC clearances dynamically during your flight.

Aircraft Management

Advanced Load & Fuel Manager

Take absolute control over your aircraft's weight and balance in real-time. Use the intuitive interactive sliders to adjust the fuel quantity (0% to 100%) in your Left, Center, and Right tanks instantly.

You can also perfectly manage your payload by entering exact weights for the Co-Pilot, Rear Passenger Seats, Baggage Hold, and Extra Cargo. Perfect your performance calculations without ever opening the simulator's default menus!

Training & Emergencies

Comprehensive Failures System

Ready for the ultimate challenge? Simulator Station features a highly advanced, study-level failure management system designed strictly for hardcore simmers and real-world training! Trigger countless realistic emergencies categorized perfectly by aircraft systems (ENGINE, APU, HYDRAULIC, ELEC, FLT CTRL, and more).

You can force an instant catastrophic failure, like an Engine Fire or a Compressor Stall, or set a precise timer for a surprise emergency mid-flight to truly test your airmanship. Things got too intense? Simply hit the incredibly powerful FIX ALL button to instantly repair the aircraft and return all systems to a 100% healthy state! This is the ultimate tool to master emergency procedures.

Configuration

Quick Settings & Account Options

Need to update your network or accounts? Head over to the Settings page to quickly modify your PC's IP Address, SimBrief ID, or VATSIM/IVAO IDs on the fly. Just tap on any field to instantly update your connection details dynamically without having to restart the application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about Simulator Station.

Why is the app showing "Disconnected" and refusing to connect?

Ensure that the Bridge server is actively running on your PC and that your mobile device and PC are on the exact same Wi-Fi network. Check that your Windows Firewall isn't blocking the connection. Crucial Step: Verify that you entered the correct PC IP Address in the app (local IPs can change upon router restarts) and ensure you are running the latest version of the Bridge.

Does the app support the new MSFS 2024?

Yes, absolutely! Simulator Station is built and optimized to fully support both MSFS 2020 and the newly released MSFS 2024 with maximum efficiency.

Why isn't my flight plan loading from SimBrief?

Ensure you have entered your correct SimBrief ID on the app's Connection page. Most importantly, you must click "Generate Flight" on the SimBrief website for your current flight before opening the Flight Plan tab in the app.

Does the map display live VATSIM and IVAO traffic?

Yes! Once you enter your VATSIM or IVAO ID on the Connection page, you can instantly view live network traffic. You can seamlessly toggle the traffic display on or off directly from the map's left-hand filter menu.

Can I use the Teleport feature while mid-air?

Yes, mid-air teleportation is fully supported. However, always ensure you configure an appropriate target Altitude and target Airspeed in the Teleport settings before executing the jump to prevent your aircraft from stalling upon arrival.
