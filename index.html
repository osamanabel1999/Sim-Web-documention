Everything You Need. All In One Place.

📍 Precision Aircraft Repositioning & World Tour

  • Dynamic Approach Setup: Instantly position your aircraft on final approach at exact distances (7, 10, or 15 nautical miles), or snap to Left/Right Base and Left/Right Downwind legs.
  • Interactive Airport Charts & Gate Spawning: View detailed, fully drawn airport charts featuring taxiways and gates. Click on any gate on the chart to instantly teleport your aircraft to that exact stand.
  • Advanced Navaid Teleportation: Search for any Navaid to view its data. Set your desired distance, altitude, airspeed, and radial, and teleport perfectly into position.
  • Scenic World Tour: Access over 30 of the world’s most breathtaking, highly detailed tourist destinations. Click and teleport instantly to enjoy stunning views without flying a full route.

🌍 Next-Gen Interactive Map & Radar

  • Live Network Traffic: Track IVAO and VATSIM flights in real-time alongside Airports, Navaids, Waypoints, Airways, Holds, FIRs, PIREPs, and your exact flight path.
  • Click-to-Teleport: Click absolutely anywhere on the map to instantly teleport your aircraft. You maintain full control over the spawned altitude, airspeed, and heading.
  • Comprehensive Data on Demand:
    • Aircraft: Click any live traffic to view full flight telemetry.
    • Airports: Click to reveal extensive data including runways, frequencies, METAR, TAF, and listen to Live Audio ATIS.
    • Waypoints/Holds: Click any navigation point to view details or teleport directly to it.
  • Customizable Map Styles: Choose from multiple map base layers to suit your flying environment.

🌤️ Immersive Aviation Weather Visualizations

  • Animated Weather Layers: Watch live weather render directly on your map, including animated rainfall effects.
  • Global Meteorological Data: Overlay real-time Wind, Temperature, Precipitation, SIGMETs, and breathtaking Aurora displays across the globe.

👨‍✈️ Virtual Crew & Performance Operations

  • Interactive Virtual Co-Pilot: Execute real-world checklists for every flight phase (Pre-flight, After Takeoff, etc.). The virtual pilot actively reads and shares the checklist workflow with you, replicating a true multi-crew environment.
  • Audio Briefings: Receive professional departure and arrival briefings from your virtual pilot.
  • Precision Performance Calculator: Generate highly accurate Takeoff and Landing performance calculations tailored to your aircraft.

✈️ Total Aircraft & Ground Control

  • Ground Handling System: Request and steer the pushback tug manually, operate the jetway, and control all aircraft doors seamlessly.
  • Advanced Load Manager: Instantly modify passenger loads, cargo weights, and fuel levels.
  • Failure Management: Trigger and manage a comprehensive suite of aircraft failures for intense training scenarios.

📱 Smart Electronic Flight Bag (EFB) & AI Integration

  • SimBrief Integration: Automatically load your flight plan, view route summaries, departure/arrival NOTAMs, and listen to Audio ATIS for both your origin and destination.
  • AI-Powered NOTAMs: Say goodbye to deciphering complex text. Our built-in AI analyzes all airport NOTAMs, provides a clean text summary, and can read it aloud to you via voice synthesis.
  • Integrated EFB iPad: Search any airport worldwide to instantly pull METAR, TAF, and categorized NOTAM statuses.
  • Aviation Scratchpads: Four dedicated digital scratchpad templates designed specifically for Takeoff, Landing, Holding, and CRAFT clearances.
